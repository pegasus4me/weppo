# Code of Conduct

Weppo is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Give feedback in good faith, respect different experiences, and take
responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation
when behavior violates this standard. Report conduct concerns privately to the
project maintainers rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
